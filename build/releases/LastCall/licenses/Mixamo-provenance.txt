The optional Don Espino Natural character uses motion downloaded from Adobe
Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com), retargeted and baked in Blender for this game.
The character source was supplied by the project owner; its separate revolver
reuses the existing project asset. Character-specific combat and moustache
actions were authored on the retargeted pose.

Source animation IDs and the distinction between Mixamo and authored actions
are documented in desgin/character_models/refined/don_espino_natural_workflow/
in the source project. Raw Mixamo FBX downloads are not included in the release.
